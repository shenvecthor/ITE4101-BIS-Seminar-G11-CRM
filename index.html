The core concept

What is a CRM System?

According to Kenneth C. Laudon and Jane P. Laudon (Management Information Systems), Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems capture and integrate customer data across an organization, consolidate and analyze it, and distribute the results to systems and customer touch points. This creates a single enterprise-wide view of customers to attract, retain, and build profitable relationships.

Three types of CRM

Operational

Runs sales, campaigns, service

Analytical

Finds segments, value, churn

Collaborative

Shares one view across teams

Step 1

Mass

One message for everyone

Step 2

Segment

One message per group

Step 3

Personal

One message per person, from past behaviour

Step 4

Predictive

The system anticipates the need first

Most Guyanese businesses sit at steps 1–2.

CRM types: Laudon & Laudon, Management Information Systems

Shein and Temu

The loop, but optimized.

Shein

Customer data designs the product

Test small. New styles launch in batches of 100–200 pieces. Live sales data decides what gets reordered.

Your own feed. Your searches and clicks shape the home page each shopper sees.

Daily habit. Points for check-ins, reviews and live streams keep you opening the app.

Temu

Shopping turned into a game

Play to save. Mini-games like Fishland and Farmland, plus spin-to-win wheels, hand out coupons and “free” gifts.

Team up. The name comes from “Team up, price down”: referral rewards turn shoppers into recruiters.

Constant nudges. Push notifications and countdown timers pull you back in.

Recent Controversy: the EU is investigating both apps for addictive design, and Temu paid Texas $3 million in October 2026 to settle a data-privacy lawsuit, without admitting wrongdoing.

Sources: Shein via Modaes; Fortune; Shein points FAQ; European Commission; Texas Attorney General

Is it still CRM?

Yes. Just extremely automated.

Why it still counts

Profiles and segments. Browsing, clicks, purchases and demographics build a profile that personalizes every screen.

Lifecycle and retention. Push alerts, abandoned-cart reminders, win-back coupons and VIP tiers all raise customer lifetime value.

Feedback into product. The analytical loop runs straight into the supply chain.

Traditional CRM

Shein and Temu

Driver

Trust, service and emotional connection

Algorithms, low prices and gamified rewards

Channel

Newsletters, loyalty tiers and support staff

Real-time pushes, dynamic feeds and bots

Goal

Long-term loyalty and advocacy

Instant sales, a daily habit and viral referrals

Most companies are hybrids. Starbucks pairs baristas who learn your order with an AI that tailors app offers to each member. The automated side is often called lifecycle marketing or algorithmic CRM.

Sources: Shein; Starbucks (Jan 2026); PYMNTS

Conclusion

Three things to remember.

01

A CRM is a memory.

Personalization is acting on that memory: collect, understand, act, learn.

02

It pays only with trust.

Relevance, timing, transparency and consent decide whether customers feel known or watched.

03

For Guyana, the moment is now.

Digital payments are creating customer data faster than the law protects it.

The vendor at Stabroek remembers you because you let them.

When a machine remembers you, did you let it?
